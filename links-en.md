# Awesome Long COVID & ME/CFS Resources – English

Curated links and resources on **Long COVID / Post-COVID**, **ME/CFS (myalgic encephalomyelitis / chronic fatigue syndrome)**, POTS, MCAS, hEDS, fibromyalgia, SIBO, dysautonomia and other post-viral conditions – with a focus on fatigue, PEM and symptom management.

For **patients, carers and researchers**. The list is multilingual: resources appear in the language in which they were published. Every entry is checked by a human before publication.

**Know a good source?** Open the [“Suggest a link” issue](https://github.com/awesome-long-covid-mecfs/awesome-long-covid-mecfs/issues/new/choose) or email `awesome-long-covid-mecfs[at]sags-per-mail.de` (replace `[at]` with `@`). Details: [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

> ⚠️ **Important:** Not medical advice and no treatment recommendation. We only link to sources – their publishers are responsible for their content.

> ℹ️ These links are language-specific: the German and English lists contain different sources, including regional services and authorities. Many research studies are published in English and are therefore more likely to appear in the English list. Choose the language that fits your region and your question.

## Tests (Questionnaires)

- **[FUNCAP – Functional Capacity](https://www.funcap.no/translations-2/funcap-in-english/)** — Free questionnaire on functional capacity in people with post-exertional malaise (PEM), available in short and full form. 🧰 [Tool]

- **[DePaul Symptom Questionnaires (incl. DSQ-PEM)](https://csh.depaul.edu/academics/centers-and-institutes/ccr/myalgic-encephalomyelitis-cfs/measures)** — Free symptom questionnaires from DePaul University, including a 10-item screen for post-exertional malaise; the page states all instruments are free to use. 🧰 [Tool] 🗂️ [Collection]

- **[10-Minute Lean Test (NASA Lean Test)](https://batemanhornecenter.org/nasa-10-minute-lean-test-2)** — Free Bateman Horne Center protocol recording blood pressure and heart rate during a 10-minute stand/lean to assess orthostatic intolerance. 🧰 [Tool]

- **[COMPASS 31](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23218087)** — Peer-reviewed 31-item patient-reported score for autonomic symptoms across six weighted domains; full text and questionnaire are freely available. 📄 [Study] 🧰 [Tool]

- **[Munich Berlin Symptom Questionnaire (MBSQ)](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/medrxiv/early/2023/08/24/2023.08.23.23293081/DC1/embed/media-1.pdf?download=true)** — Clinician-administered ME/CFS symptom questionnaire (PDF, [mirror](https://akureyrarklinikin.is/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Munich-Berlin-Symptom-Questionnaire.pdf)); the [study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38095713) describes its use. 🧰 [Tool] 📄 [Study]

- **[Bell Score](https://www.oiresource.com/cfsscale.htm)** — 0–100 functional-status scale for ME/CFS; the freely accessible copy is reproduced with the author's permission ([online tool](https://meandcfs.org/tools/bell-scale)). 🧰 [Tool]

- **[PROMIS](https://healthmeasures.net/promis-basics/)** — Patient-reported health measures from the NIH-funded HealthMeasures initiative, including fatigue; free for individual clinical and research use, electronic use requires permission. 🧰 [Tool]

- **[WHODAS 2.0](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health/who-disability-assessment-schedule)** — WHO disability assessment instrument with 12- and 36-item versions across six functioning domains; paper use is free, electronic use requires a licence. 🏛️ [Authority] 🧰 [Tool]

- **[Post-COVID Functional Status Scale (PCFS)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7236834)** — Study describing a scale for functional status and everyday limitations after COVID-19. 📄 [Study] 🧰 [Tool]

## Research & Studies

- **[ME/CFS Research Summaries](https://hanneseh.github.io/ME-CFS-Research-Summaries/en/)** — Research tracker with plain-language summaries and direct links to primary sources. 🗂️ [Collection]

- **[COVID Studies (Zotero group library)](https://www.zotero.org/groups/5006109/covidstudies/library)** — Public group library collecting COVID-related studies; no login required. 🗂️ [Collection]

- **[OpenSource Med](https://opensourcemed.info/)** — Independent open-access research initiative with a daily-updated tracker ([source code](https://github.com/MattH55/osmf-research-tracker)), research notes and tools for candidate compounds, biomarkers and registered trials in Long COVID and other post-viral conditions. 🗂️ [Collection] 🧰 [Tool]

- **[MEpedia](https://me-pedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_MEpedia)** — Patient-written encyclopedia on ME/CFS and related topics, founded by the MEAction Network; articles are freely readable without an account. 🤝 [Self-help] 🗂️ [Collection]

## Treatment & Therapy

- **[RTHM](https://www.rthm.com/)** — AI-based platform for Long COVID, ME/CFS, MCAS, POTS and related conditions where users can upload health data and lab results for a personalized health roadmap; AI use and lab-result review are free, while video consultations, lab tests and medications are paid and some services are limited to the US. 🧰 [Tool] 🆓 [Partly free] 💰 [Commercial]

## Self-Help & Community

- **[CrunchME](https://crunchme.org/)** — Data-informed advocacy for ME/CFS, Long COVID and other infection-associated chronic illnesses. 📣 [Advocacy]

- **[ME/CFS Useful Resources](https://github.com/me-cfs/me-cfs.github.io/blob/main/useful-resources.md)** — Community-curated collection of diagnostic criteria, communities and further ME/CFS resources. 🗂️ [Collection]

## Patient Organisations

- **[#MEAction](https://www.meaction.net/)** — International patient network on ME and Long COVID with campaigns, group and clinician directories and support resources; sign-up is only needed for newsletters and events. 🤝 [Self-help] 📣 [Advocacy]

- **[The ME Association](https://meassociation.org.uk/)** — UK charity providing a free helpline, fact sheets and local groups for people with ME/CFS. 🤝 [Self-help]

- **[Action for ME](https://www.actionforme.org.uk/)** — UK charity offering support services, forums and under-18 support for people with ME/CFS. 🤝 [Self-help]

- **[Long Covid Kids](https://www.longcovidkids.org/)** — UK charity supporting children and young people with Long COVID and ME and their families, with an education toolkit. 🤝 [Self-help]

- **[Patient-Led Research Collaborative](https://patientresearchcovid19.com/)** — International group of Long COVID patients conducting patient-led research and publishing plain-language resources. 🤝 [Self-help] 🗂️ [Collection]

- **[Solve M.E.](https://solvecfs.org/)** — US nonprofit offering patient and caregiver resources and funding research on ME/CFS and Long COVID. 🤝 [Self-help] 📣 [Advocacy]

- **[Dysautonomia International](https://www.dysautonomiainternational.org/)** — Nonprofit for autonomic disorders including POTS, with support-group and clinician finders and Long COVID research funding. 🤝 [Self-help]

- **[The Ehlers-Danlos Society](https://www.ehlers-danlos.com/)** — International nonprofit with support-group and charity directories and resources on hEDS/HSD, fatigue and dysautonomia. 🤝 [Self-help]

- **[COVID-19 Longhauler Advocacy Project](https://www.longhauler-advocacy.org/)** — US nonprofit offering a patient guide, community and advocacy for Long COVID. 🤝 [Self-help] 📣 [Advocacy]
